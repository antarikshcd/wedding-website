Travel Tips

Everything you need to plan your stay and enjoy India to the fullest!

💡 Visa information

    E-Visa Application:
  • Every non-Indian citizen requires to apply for a e-Visa for entry into India. Do not use any other website, as the e Visa should be applied only through the authorized Indian government website . Apply for the e-Visa atleast 14 days in advance of your travel date.

    • E-Arrival Card:
  • In addition, to the e-Visa you will need to fill an e-Arrival Card which is available through the authorized website , atleast 72 hours prior to travel.

🚗 Transportation Advice

    Airports:
  • The nearest airport is Dabolim International Airport (GOI). Best way to get to Goa from Europe, however, is with Qatar airways that lands at Manohar International Airport (GOX), which is located in North Goa and is a bit far from the wedding venue. Alternately, you can land in New Delhi, Mumbai or Bangalore and take connecting flights from there.

    • Trains:
  • Trains to the rest of India can be taken from Madgaon Railway Station, located in city of Margao, which is the closest big city to the wedding venue. Trains can be reserved at the authorized Indian Railways website: IRCTC . Normally, train bookings open 60 days in advance of the travel date. But for an additional charge, as non-Indian citizens, you can choose to reserve the tickets 365 days in advance of the travel date. It is highly recommended that you reserve train travel in advance as a last minute seat won't be guaranteed.

    • Local Travel in Goa:
  • Public transport is almost non-existent or not recommended. Use prepaid taxis or the taxi apps GoaMiles and GoaTaxi for local travel. NOTE: GoaMiles and GoaTaxi are the only apps for cabs that works in Goa. Unlike rest of India Uber doesn't operate here.
  • Rental scooters and cars are easy to find for sightseeing. But if you are uncomfortable with the notion of driving in India, then a taxi with a driver for the entire day is another option.

    • Local travel in rest of India:
  • Taxi apps like Uber and Ola are widely available. Additionally, you can find auto-rickshaws and local taxis in many cities. The local taxis and auto-rickshaws might not be metered, so brush up your haggling skills and decide the fares before getting in.

💡 General Tips

    Payment methods in India:
    • 💰 Cash works in all places and it is recommended to carry some cash (around 10,000 INR ≈ 100 Euros) with you.
    • 💳 If you have a WISE or Revolut Debit card, it can be used at all places where Debit cards are accepted (including all travel bookings).
    • 📱 Alternately, the mobile payment system in India "UPI" has recently been opened to non-Indian citizens. More information can be found on this website.

    Wedding attire:
  • For the wedding and pre-wedding events feel free to wear western clothing as there is no obligation to wear Indian traditional attire. In case, you are still interested in wearing Indian attire, you can check out the shops in the market area of Margao or Panaji cities.

    • Mobile connectivity:
  • European sims work in India with International roaming plans. In case, you want to buy an Indian sim card, you can do so at your arrival airport (Airtel, VI or Jio have good network coverage).

    • Food and water:
  • Stick to bottled or filtered water. Street food is good but choose stalls with high footfalls, and which in your opinion maintain satisfactory hygiene levels. Not all food in India is spicy and you can find sufficient options for non-spicy cuisine.

    • Clothing:
  • Dress modestly in places of religious worship (shorts/skirts are not allowed at many places).
