Travel Tips

Everything you need to plan your stay and enjoy Goa to the fullest!

🏨 Hotel Recommendations

  • Hotel Laxmi Empire – Near Madgaon railway station. Margao is the closest big city from the wedding venue at a 15 min drive. Transportation from this hotel to the wedding venue will be provided by us for 21st February.
  • Regenta Baywatch Resort Goa – For those who prefer beachfront accommodation. It's a 5-10 min walk from the wedding venue and is right by Sernabatim beach.
  • Taj Exotica – A luxury resort option at Benaulim beach. It's a 13 min drive from the wedding venue.

🚗 Transportation Advice

    Airports:
  • The nearest airport is Dabolim International Airport (GOI). Best way to get to Goa from Europe, however, is with Qatar airways that lands at Manohar International Airport (GOX), which is located in North Goa and is a bit far from the wedding venue. Alternately, you can land in New Delhi, Mumbai or Bangalore and take connecting flights from there.
    • Local Travel in Goa:
  • Public transport is almost non-existent or not recommended. Use prepaid taxis or the taxi app GoaMiles for local travel. NOTE: GoaMiles is the only app for cabs that works in Goa. Unlike rest of India Uber doesn't operate here.
    • Trains:
  • Trains to the rest of India can be taken from Madgaon Railway Station, located in city of Margao, which is the closest big city to the wedding venue.
    • Sightseeing travel:
  • Rental scooters and cars are easy to find for sightseeing. But if you are uncomfortable with the notion of driving in India, then a taxi with a driver for the entire day is another option.

💡 General Tips

  • Carry sunscreen, sunglasses, and light cotton clothes.
  • Keep some cash handy; smaller shops may not accept cards.
  • Respect local culture and beach etiquette.
  • Stay hydrated and enjoy responsibly!
